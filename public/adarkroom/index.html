📖 KAISE KHELE (HOW TO PLAY)
🔥
1. Stoke Fire: Aag ko jalaye rakhein taaki kamra garm rahe aur bhatke hue travelers aapke paas aayein.
🌲
2. Gather Wood: Silent Forest se lakdi ikatthi karein (Stores me wood badhega).
🛠
3. Build & Craft: Cart, traps, aur huts banayein taaki log aapki basti me sharan lekar kaam karein.
🗺
4. Explore World: Compass aur weapons craft karke andheri duniya ke raaz explore karein!
