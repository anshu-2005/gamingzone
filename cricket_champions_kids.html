📖 How to Play?

  • Shot Timing Meter: Tap your shot when the needle hits the green center zone for maximum Six and Four probability!
  • Batting Controls: Tap the big colorful buttons or use keyboard keys 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or [D] to Defend.
  • Powerplay: The opening overs give field bonuses for easy boundaries.
  • Bowling: Use Fast, Spin, Yorker or Bouncer to outfox the batsman!
  • Super Over: If the match ends in a tie, a thrilling 1-over Super Over will decide the winner!
