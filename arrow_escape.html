How to Play

Master the rules in 3 simple steps.

1️⃣
Tap to Slide: Arrows slide in the direction of their arrowhead. They must exit the board completely.
2️⃣
Clear Paths Only: An arrow can only escape if the path between its tip and the board border is totally empty.
3️⃣
Watch Your Lives: Tapping a blocked arrow costs 1 life. Lose 3 lives and you must retry!
